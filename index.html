Piri-Piri Chicken Rice & Bean Bowls

35 min4 portions45–50g protein
Ingredients
  • 650g skinless chicken breast, cut into 2.5cm cubes
  • 300g dry long-grain rice
  • 1 × 400g tin kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 red pepper (~150g), cut into 1.5cm pieces
  • 1 medium brown onion (~150g), cut into 1cm dice
  • 2 medium carrots (~160g total), peeled and sliced into 3–4mm half-moons
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
  • 150g high-protein Greek-style yoghurt
  • 15ml (1 tbsp) vegetable or olive oil
  • Chicken seasoning: 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp garlic granules, 1 tsp dried oregano, ½ tsp chilli powder, ¾ tsp fine salt, ½ tsp black pepper
  • Yoghurt sauce: 150g yoghurt, 1 garlic clove, ½ tsp smoked paprika, ¼ tsp salt, 1 tsp lemon juice and 1–2 tsp cold water
Method
  1. Rinse 300g rice under cold water. Add it to a saucepan with 600ml cold water and ½ tsp salt. Bring to the boil, cover, reduce to the lowest heat and cook for 12 minutes. Turn the heat off and leave covered for 8 minutes.
  2. Toss the chicken cubes with 15ml oil, 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp garlic granules, 1 tsp oregano, ½ tsp chilli powder, ¾ tsp salt and ½ tsp black pepper.
  3. Heat a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken for 8–10 minutes, turning regularly, until browned and cooked through. Remove to a plate.
  4. Add the onion and carrots to the same pan and cook for 4 minutes. Add the pepper and cook for 3–4 minutes. Add the drained kidney beans and cook for 2 minutes.
  5. Mix the yoghurt sauce ingredients until smooth. Divide the rice, vegetables, beans and chicken between four bowls and spoon roughly 35–40g sauce over each.

Turkey Kofta & Crispy Potato Traybake

55 min4 portions~40g protein
Ingredients
  • 500g lean turkey mince
  • 1kg potatoes, cut into even 2cm cubes
  • 1 red pepper, cut into 2cm pieces
  • 1 medium brown onion: half cut into very fine 3–4mm dice and half sliced into 5mm strips
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
  • 200g high-protein Greek-style yoghurt
  • 30ml (2 tbsp) oil
  • Potatoes: 1½ tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp garlic granules, ½ tsp dried oregano, ¾ tsp salt, ¼ tsp black pepper
  • Koftas: 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp ground cumin, ½ tsp dried oregano, ½ tsp salt, ¼ tsp black pepper
  • Garlic yoghurt: 200g yoghurt, 1 garlic clove, ¼ tsp salt, ¼ tsp black pepper and 1 tsp lemon juice
Method
  1. Heat the oven to 220°C conventional / 200°C fan / Gas 7.
  2. Toss the 2cm potato cubes with 22.5ml (1½ tbsp) oil, 1½ tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp garlic granules, ½ tsp oregano, ¾ tsp salt and ¼ tsp black pepper. Roast for 20 minutes.
  3. Mix the turkey mince, finely diced half-onion, 1 grated garlic clove, egg, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp oregano, ½ tsp salt and ¼ tsp black pepper only until combined.
  4. Divide the mixture into 12 pieces of roughly 45g and shape each into a kofta about 7cm long and 2.5cm thick.
  5. Turn the potatoes. Add the pepper, sliced onion and remaining 7.5ml oil. Add the koftas with gaps between them and roast for 20–25 minutes, turning the koftas after about 12 minutes.
  6. Mix the yoghurt, remaining garlic, ¼ tsp salt, ¼ tsp pepper and 1 tsp lemon juice. Serve three koftas per person with one-quarter of the potatoes and vegetables.

Lean Beef & Bean Tomato Spaghetti

45 min4 portions40–45g protein
Ingredients
  • 500g 5%-fat beef mince
  • 350g dry spaghetti
  • 1 × 400g tin kidney beans, drained
  • 2 × 400g tins chopped tomatoes
  • 1 medium brown onion, cut into 5mm dice
  • 1 medium carrot (~80g), coarsely grated
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
  • 250g frozen mixed vegetables
  • 15ml (1 tbsp) oil
  • 15g (1 tbsp) tomato purée
  • 1½ tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ¼ tsp chilli flakes
Method
  1. Heat 15ml oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onion and carrot and cook for 5 minutes. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
  2. Increase the heat to medium-high. Add the beef and cook for 6–8 minutes, breaking it into small pieces until properly browned.
  3. Stir in 15g tomato purée and cook for 1 minute. Add the tomatoes, kidney beans, frozen vegetables, oregano, basil, paprika, salt, black pepper, chilli flakes and 100ml water.
  4. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered for 20 minutes, stirring every few minutes. Add another 50ml water only if it becomes too thick.
  5. Cook 350g spaghetti in well-salted boiling water until al dente according to the packet, usually 9–11 minutes. Reserve 100ml pasta water before draining.
  6. Combine the pasta and sauce. Add 30–60ml pasta water as required so the sauce evenly coats the spaghetti.
Thursday

Sticky Chicken Egg-Fried Rice

35 min4 portions45–50g protein
Ingredients
  • 650g chicken breast, cut into 2cm cubes
  • 300g dry long-grain rice
  • 500g frozen mixed vegetables
  • 3 large eggs
  • 3 garlic cloves, finely grated
  • 15ml (1 tbsp) vegetable oil
  • Chicken: 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp garlic granules, ½ tsp black pepper and ¼ tsp salt
  • Sauce: 45ml soy sauce, 30ml sriracha, 15ml water, 1 tsp honey or brown sugar and ½ tsp garlic granules
Method
  1. Cook 300g rice with 600ml water using the covered absorption method: 12 minutes on the lowest heat, then 8 minutes resting off the heat. Spread the cooked rice over a large plate or tray for 10 minutes so excess steam escapes.
  2. Season the chicken with 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp garlic granules, ½ tsp black pepper and ¼ tsp salt.
  3. Mix 45ml soy sauce, 30ml sriracha, 15ml water, 1 tsp honey or brown sugar and ½ tsp garlic granules.
  4. Heat a large frying pan or wok over medium-high heat. Add 7.5ml oil and cook the chicken for 8–10 minutes. Remove.
  5. Add the remaining 7.5ml oil and frozen vegetables. Fry for 5–6 minutes until hot and most surface moisture has evaporated. Add the garlic for 30 seconds.
  6. Push the vegetables aside. Scramble the eggs for 60–90 seconds. Add the rice, increase to high heat and stir-fry for 3–4 minutes.
  7. Return the chicken, pour in the sauce and toss for 1–2 minutes until everything is evenly coated.
Friday

Turkey, Bean & Cheese Quesadillas

30 min4 portions40–45g protein
Ingredients
  • 500g lean turkey mince
  • 8 large flour tortilla wraps
  • 1 × 400g tin kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 red pepper, cut into 1cm dice
  • 1 medium brown onion, cut into 5mm dice
  • 160g mature cheddar, grated
  • 150g high-protein Greek-style yoghurt
  • 15ml (1 tbsp) oil
  • Turkey seasoning: 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp ground cumin, ½ tsp garlic granules, ½ tsp chilli powder, ½ tsp dried oregano, ¾ tsp salt and ¼ tsp black pepper
  • Yoghurt dip: 150g yoghurt, ½ tsp smoked paprika, ¼ tsp garlic granules and ¼ tsp salt
Method
  1. Heat 15ml oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the onion and pepper and cook for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the turkey mince, break it into small pieces and cook for 7–8 minutes.
  3. Add 2 tsp paprika, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp garlic granules, ½ tsp chilli powder, ½ tsp oregano, ¾ tsp salt and ¼ tsp pepper. Cook for 1 minute.
  4. Add the kidney beans and cook for 2 minutes, lightly crushing roughly one-third with the back of a spoon.
  5. Divide the filling between four tortillas, stopping 2cm from the edge. Sprinkle exactly 40g cheddar over each and top with another tortilla.
  6. Cook each quesadilla in a clean dry frying pan over medium heat for 2½–3 minutes per side. Rest for 2 minutes before cutting. Mix the yoghurt dip and serve 35–40g per person.
